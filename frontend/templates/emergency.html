Urgent help

Emergency and safety information

If you are experiencing a serious or life-threatening reaction, seek immediate professional/emergency medical help.

SmartMed Companion is an educational and medication-support tool. It does not replace advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a doctor or pharmacist.
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe swelling
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe chest pain
  • Severe confusion
  • Serious allergic reaction symptoms

Do not attempt self-diagnosis or medication changes without speaking to a qualified healthcare professional.
